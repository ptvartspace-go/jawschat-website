CEFR Placement Test

What this is: a 30-question adaptive placement test that estimates your English level on the CEFR scale, from A1 (beginner) to C2 (proficient).

How it works: the test adjusts as you go. Answer well and the questions get harder; struggle and they get easier — so it homes in on your real level with far fewer questions than a fixed test.

Choose the best answer for each question. You can't go back, and there's no time limit — expect about 10–15 minutes. Your result appears at the end with a level, a score, and a breakdown by skill.
